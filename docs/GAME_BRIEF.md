# Game Brief

# Goblin: Last Stop to Montauk

## Short Version

During a raccoon-borne outbreak that has taken over New York City, one trash panda named Goblin runs a tiny repair shop inside a post-apocalyptic subway station.

Goblin helps the remaining creatures of the city repair tools, build escape gear, avoid infection, and make their way toward the safety of Montauk.

The game is a cute, strange, top-down 2D survival adventure about scavenging, repairing, helping odd characters, and trying to escape a city swallowed by raccoon chaos.

## Expanded Premise

New York City has fallen to a mysterious virus spread by raccoons.

The streets are unsafe. The subway tunnels are half-collapsed. The stations are filled with broken machines, abandoned luggage, infected creatures, strange survivors, and piles of useful junk.

Deep under the city, in a forgotten subway station, lives **Goblin** — a small trash panda with a repair bench, a suspicious amount of confidence, and a dream of reaching Montauk.

Goblin is not the strongest survivor. He is not the fastest. He is definitely not medically qualified.

But he can fix things.

As creatures from across the city pass through the station, Goblin helps them repair broken tools, build protective gear, trade supplies, and prepare for the dangerous journey east.

To survive, Goblin must explore subway tunnels and surface ruins, collect scrap and parts, repair useful items, avoid infection, help other animals, and slowly construct an escape plan.

The world is falling apart.

The repair shop is barely standing.

The customers are weird.

The tools are questionable.

Montauk is far away.

Goblin gets to work.

## Genre

Top-down 2D cute survival adventure.

The game combines:

- Light survival
- Exploration
- Repair/shopkeeping mechanics
- Fetch quests
- Crafting
- Creature interactions
- Simple infection risk
- Strange humor
- Small narrative choices

## Intended Tone
